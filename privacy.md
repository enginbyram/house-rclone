# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

house rclone is used only by its owner, Engin Bayram. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive account, with the owner's permission, to sync and back up the owner's own files between that Drive and the owner's own devices.

No data is collected from anyone else. No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. Files only move between the owner's Google Drive and computers the owner controls.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact: via the GitHub profile [enginbyram](https://github.com/enginbyram).
